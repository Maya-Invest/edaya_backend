# Work remaining beyond this staging release

This staging release does not complete or qualify the v1 feature program. The authoritative scope remains the [common requirements](../sprints/v1/00-common-requirements.md) and its five feature plans. Recheck their implementation/evidence status against the final v1 candidate; the items below are not claims that every underlying feature is absent.

- [Guardian pairing and invitations](../sprints/v1/01-guardian-pairing-and-invitations.md): full onboarding, verified identity, multiple guardians/children, real native links, interruption/recovery, and revocation qualification.
- [Student photos and branding](../sprints/v1/02-student-photos-and-branding.md): approval/rejection lifecycle, publication continuity, private/public asset boundaries, processing failures, and actual client/document output.
- [Service catalog and accounting](../sprints/v1/03-service-catalog-and-accounting.md): the new account/charge/allocation/reversal/refund model and reconciliation. Passing current finance tests does not qualify this new ledger.
- [Official documents](../sprints/v1/04-official-documents.md): all document groups, immutable issuance/number history, actual PDF rendering and visual checks, authorized downloads, and interrupted-render recovery.
- [Family feedback surveys](../sprints/v1/05-family-feedback-surveys.md): collection, closure, moderation, confidentiality, threshold suppression, teacher-safe aggregates, and reminder races.
- [Final coordinated v1 rehearsal](../sprints/v1/06-final-release-rehearsal.md): all predecessor gates, two-school adversarial scenarios, real-device links and PDFs, accounting reconciliation, restoration, operational support, and final matching-revision evidence.

The older [release-candidate plan](../sprints/backend-release-candidate-plan.md) also retains unfinished launch qualification: complete privileged-path/idempotency and audit review, full authentication lifecycle/cookie topology, external consent and restricted-scope client journeys, restore/retention/repair operations, and resolution of launch-blocking defects. Its supersession notes govern identity and the new finance work.

Production deployment, iOS artifacts, Google Play/App Store publication, and production cutover are outside this staging assignment.
